### 📦 Product Line Matrix:

1. **Security Questionnaire Copilot (First High-Friction Entry Point):**
   - Auto-fills CAIQ, SIG, ISO 27001, SOC 2 forms with 100% evidence audit trails (40 hrs ➔ 2 hrs).
2. **Enterprise Knowledge Copilot (Multi-Doc RAG):**
   - Indexes unstructured PDF, SOP, ISO, MES, and SAP internal corporate manuals for zero-hallucination Q&A.
3. **Proposal & Bid Copilot (RFP Automation):**
   - Cross-references past winning bids to generate compliant tender proposals for government and SI bids.
4. **Supplier Compliance & ESG Copilot:**
   - Extracts carbon metrics, labor policies, and EcoVadis CSRD disclosures from supplier PDF packets.
5. **Physical AI & Robotics Gateway:**
   - Connects ROS 2 3D spatial vision coordinates to Web REST APIs for industrial robot control.
6. **AI Workflow Studio:**
   - Integrates n8n, Slack, Gmail, and ERP/SAP approval workflows.
