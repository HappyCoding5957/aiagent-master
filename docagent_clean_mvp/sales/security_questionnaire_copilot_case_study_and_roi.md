## 📈 1. Enterprise Case Study (Anonymized Mid-Market SaaS Vendor)

### 🏢 Client Background
- **Profile:** B2B Cloud SaaS Vendor (200 employees, SOC 2 Type II Certified).
- **Challenge:** Receives 15–25 enterprise vendor security questionnaires per month (CAIQ, SIG, ISO 27001, NIST, custom Excel forms). Senior Security Engineers spent 40+ hours per month copy-pasting answers from internal policy PDFs.
- **Sales Impact:** Deals were delayed by 2–3 weeks waiting for security approval sign-off.

### 🚀 The Solution (Security Questionnaire Copilot)
- **Deployment:** 2-Week Private Cloud Container Deployment.
- **Workflow:** Security team drops policy PDFs into Slack/Drive. Copilot indexes policy chunks into `pgvector` with exact line citations.
- **Results:**
  - **Auto-Fill Rate:** 88% of questionnaire items answered automatically with >90% confidence scores.
  - **Time Saved:** Questionnaire processing time reduced from **40 hours ➔ 2 hours** per month.
  - **Sales Cycle Speed:** Sales deal turnaround accelerated by **14 days**.
  - **Accuracy:** Zero false compliance claims (100% evidence-backed citations).
